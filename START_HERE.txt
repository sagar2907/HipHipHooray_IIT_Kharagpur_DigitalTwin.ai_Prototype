  python -m pytest tests/ -q

  and they run too, giving 12/12.

IMPORTANT — THE DATASETS ARE NOT IN THE REPO
===============================================================
  ~765 MB of generated data is deliberately gitignored. The
  generators are what is versioned, and they take fixed seeds, so
  everything reproduces bit-for-bit. Regenerate with the builders
  in scripts/. Budget a couple of hours for the full v5 truth
  build.

===============================================================
THE ONE THING TO UNDERSTAND
===============================================================
  Everything we have today is BATCH — scripts that read a finished
  dataset and print a score. That is a digital shadow, not a twin.
  Closing the loop so it ingests, detects, ranks and re-reads on a
  timer is the whole difference, and it is the claim a Round 2
  judge will probe hardest. It is Phase 3 in the plan, and the
  plan says: if time runs short, cut anything else first.
