Reading this honestly. Absolute error does shrink as damage accumulates
(the estimate sharpens), but it stays biased LONG throughout, because
real degradation accelerates faster than the fitted curve. Percentage
error rises only because the remaining horizon shrinks toward zero.

Product conclusion: do NOT show operators a precise countdown - it
would be systematically over-optimistic and would get someone burned.
Alert on detection, which is reliable, and show a coarse band such as
'hours, not minutes'. Prognosis is the weakest link in this pipeline
and saying so is better than being caught claiming otherwise.

The sudden-shift tool is excluded deliberately: no method can forecast
a step change, and claiming otherwise would be dishonest.
